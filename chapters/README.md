# Computational Skepticism for AI: chapter guide

[Return to the book overview](../README.md).

### Part I · Ground a claim in evidence

1. [The Skeptic's Toolkit](../chapters/01-the-skeptics-toolkit.md): Use the skeptic’s toolkit to separate a plausible artifact from evidence about the world.
2. [Probability, Uncertainty, and the Confidence Illusion](../chapters/02-probability-uncertainty-and-the-confidence-illusion.md): Distinguish uncertainty in the world from a confidence number printed by a model.
3. [Data Validation: Reconstructing the Epistemic Frame Behind a Dataset](../chapters/03-data-validation-reconstructing-the-epistemic-frame-behind-a-dataset.md): Reconstruct how a dataset was collected and what it can represent.

### Part II · Test the model and its effects

4. [Robustness: What "Understanding" Means When a Pixel Can Break the Model](../chapters/04-robustness-what-understanding-means-when-a-pixel-can-break-the-model.md): Test which input changes can break an apparently reliable result.
5. [Model Explainability: Distinguishing Explanation from the Appearance of Explanation](../chapters/05-model-explainability-distinguishing-explanation-from-the-appearance-of-explanation.md): Distinguish a useful explanation from one that merely sounds convincing.
6. [Bias: Where It Enters and Who Is Responsible](../chapters/06-bias-where-it-enters-and-who-is-responsible.md): Locate where bias entered and who can act on it.
7. [Fairness Metrics: Choosing a Definition and Defending It](../chapters/07-fairness-metrics-choosing-a-definition-and-defending-it.md): Choose and defend a fairness definition for the decision at hand.

### Part III · Supervise agents and communicate decisions

8. [Validating Agentic AI: When Autonomous Systems Misbehave](../chapters/08-validating-agentic-ai-when-autonomous-systems-misbehave.md): Validate an agent’s observed actions, not its account of what happened.
9. [Delegation, Trust, and the Supervisory Role](../chapters/09-delegation-trust-and-the-supervisory-role.md): Retain a named human owner and define the terms of delegation.
10. [Visualization Under Validation: Honest, Misleading, and the Choices Between](../chapters/10-visualization-under-validation-honest-misleading-and-the-choices-between.md): Audit whether a visualization shows the comparison its caption claims.
11. [Communicating Uncertainty: Calibrating Claims to Evidence](../chapters/11-communicating-uncertainty-calibrating-claims-to-evidence.md): Communicate uncertainty at the level the evidence supports.

### Part IV · Accountability and limits

12. [Accountability: Who Is Responsible When the System Fails?](../chapters/12-accountability-who-is-responsible-when-the-system-fails.md): Assign responsibility before a failure, then trace what reviewers could have known.
13. [The Limits of AI: What the Tools Cannot Do](../chapters/13-the-limits-of-ai-what-the-tools-cannot-do.md): Recognize where available tools cannot answer a question and how to abstain.
